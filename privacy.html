Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Ui Books is a free reading website hosted on GitHub Pages. This policy explains what information may be processed when you use the website.

Information stored on your device

Without signing in, reading preferences, saved-book selections, reading activity and study goals may be stored in your browser's local storage. This data remains on your device unless you clear browser storage.

Accounts and Supabase

If you create an account or sign in, Supabase processes authentication information such as your email address and account identifier. The site may also store your public display name and avatar, book ratings, reviews, comments, reports, saved books and reading progress. Your email address is not displayed publicly.

Google and Facebook sign-in are optional. If enabled by the site operator, those providers share account information according to the permissions shown during sign-in and their own privacy policies.

Public contributions

Book reviews, comments, replies, display names and avatars may be visible to other visitors. Do not publish sensitive personal information. Content may be moderated or removed under the site's rules.

Hosting and third-party services

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Fonts may be loaded from Google Fonts, the Supabase JavaScript library from jsDelivr, and account/database services from Supabase. Those providers may receive technical information such as IP address and browser request metadata when your browser connects to them. Their own privacy policies apply.

Cookies, storage and analytics

Ui Books does not intentionally use advertising trackers or third-party analytics. Supabase authentication may use browser storage and necessary session mechanisms to keep you signed in.

Your choices

You can use the reading experience without creating an account. You can clear local browser storage to remove locally saved preferences. Signed-in users can edit their display name, delete their own reviews and comments where available, and request account deletion using the account controls. Account deletion removes the account and associated private library/rating data; public contributions may be retained in anonymized form as described in the application.

Children's privacy

Ui Books is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact the site operator through the repository's issue tracker.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the site changes. The latest version will always be published at this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the public issue tracker. Do not include passwords, authentication codes, private account details or other sensitive information in public issues.
