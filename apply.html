Write for Ui Books

Share your stories with readers who love fantasy, adventure and folklore.

How it works

  1. Apply below with your pen name and a short writing sample.
  2. We review your application. You can see its status on this page.
  3. Write in your own author dashboard: add books, chapters and a cover.
  4. Submit a finished book. After a quick check, it is published with its own page, a place in the library and your author page.

What we ask

  • Only original work that you own the rights to.
  • No hateful, violent-extremist or sexually explicit content.
  • We may remove content that breaks these rules.
