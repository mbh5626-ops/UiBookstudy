Copyright Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Ui Books is run by Lantern Light. We respect the work of writers and artists, and we expect everyone who publishes here to do the same. This page explains how to report content that copies your work, and what happens next.

Report a copyright problem

Email mbh5626@gmail.com with the subject "Copyright notice" and include:

  1. Your full name and contact details (email, and a postal address or phone number if you can).
  2. A description of your work that you say was copied, and, if it is online, a link to it.
  3. The link (URL) of each page on Ui Books that has the copied material.
  4. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorized by you, the copyright owner, or the law.
  5. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate, and that you are the copyright owner or are authorized to act for the owner.
  6. Your signature (typing your full name is enough).

Please send a complete notice. Incomplete notices may delay our response.

What we do

We read each complete notice and look at the content. If the complaint is valid, we remove the content or disable access to it quickly. We tell the author who published it and may share your notice with them, including your name and contact details, so they can respond. We may also ask search engines to drop the removed pages.

If your work was removed (counter-notice)

If you are an author and you believe your work was removed by mistake, or you have permission to use it, email mbh5626@gmail.com with the subject "Counter-notice" and include:

  1. Your name, contact details and signature.
  2. A description of the content that was removed and where it appeared.
  3. A statement that you believe in good faith that the content was removed by mistake or misidentification.
  4. A statement that you agree to receive further messages about the dispute at the contact details you gave.

We will pass your counter-notice to the person who complained. If they do not tell us within 10 business days that they have started legal action, we may restore the content.

Repeat infringement

Authors who repeatedly publish content that copies other people's work lose their author access, and their accounts may be closed.

False claims

Please only send a notice if you are sure. Knowingly making a false claim about copyright can have legal consequences.

Other problems

For other problems with content, such as harassment or inappropriate material, use the Report link next to a review or comment, or write to mbh5626@gmail.com. See also our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
